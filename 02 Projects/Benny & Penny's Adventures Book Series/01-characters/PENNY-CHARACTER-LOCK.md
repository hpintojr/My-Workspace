## Core identity (LOCKED)

- **Age shown:** 3–5 years old — a young preschooler. Never older than early elementary age.
- **Proportions:** Young-child build, soft and round rather than lanky. A little leaner than a 2–3-year-old toddler but still clearly preschool-age — head-to-body ratio slightly less exaggerated than a toddler's.
- **Cheeks:** Round soft cheeks, gentle rosy blush.
- **Face:** Soft, youthful features. Small **button nose**. Sweet, curious smile.
- **Eyes:** Large brown eyes.
- **Hair:** Dark brown, **straight**. Two **low pigtails**. Soft bangs across the forehead.
- **Bows:** **Mint green** bows on the pigtails (one per pigtail).
- **Skin tone:** Warm light-to-medium (match approved references; keep consistent page to page).
- **Personality:** Curious, brave, sweet, gentle.
- **Constant companion:** Almost always holding, hugging, or seated beside **Benny Bear** when the scene allows.

## Wardrobe (LOCKED defaults)

- **Signature outfit:** Mint-green dress with a small white **heart pattern**. White sneakers with mint accents and white socks. *(This is her cover/title look and the default unless a page calls for something else.)*
- **Infusion-day comfort look:** Soft **mint pajamas or comfy play clothes** + a cozy mint blanket (used on the "getting ready / during infusion" pages). Still recognizably Penny — same hair, bows, face.
- Do not change the default dress design, bow color, pigtail placement, eye color, facial proportions, or shoe color without a page-specific, author-approved reason.

## Expression range (for page briefs)

Happy/excited · curious/interested · a little nervous-but-safe · calm/relaxed · brave/proud · peaceful/sleepy. Penny **may look nervous**, but she is always clearly safe and supported — never scared, crying hard, or in distress.

## DO NOT DRIFT (hard rules)

- ❌ Do not age her up — no older-child / "big kid" proportions or face beyond early elementary age.
- ❌ Do not change pigtails to a different style, move them, or drop the bows.
- ❌ Do not change bow color (mint), eye color (brown), or the button nose.
- ❌ Do not restyle the heart-pattern dress on her default/cover look.
- ❌ Do not change her sneakers away from white with mint accents on her default look.
- ❌ Do not make her skin tone darker or lighter than the approved reference.
- ✅ Keep her clearly younger than the patients a nurse would usually see — she reads as a sweet young preschooler.

## Personality & world context (added 2026-08-24 — merged from Copilot draft, reconciled against locked visuals)

**Theme:** Bravery, curiosity, emotional growth, medical empowerment.
**Signature phrase:** "It's okay to feel nervous. I am brave. I can do this."

**Core traits:** Brave (even when scared) · curious · empathetic · playful · honest about her feelings · imaginative.
**Strengths:** Asks questions · connects emotionally with others · finds comfort in routines · builds trust with caregivers.
**Challenges:** Nervous about medical procedures · overthinks new experiences · needs reassurance · sensitive to tone and environment.
**Motivations:** Feeling safe · understanding what's happening · helping Benny Bear "be brave" · connecting with Nurse Ivy · learning new things.
**Fears:** Needles · loud medical equipment · being alone during infusion · unexpected changes.

**Relationships**
- **Benny Bear:** comfort object and emotional anchor — Penny projects her feelings onto Benny, and he helps her express bravery.
- **Nurse Ivy:** trusted adult, gentle and patient, represents safety and empowerment.
- **Device helpers** (Polly Pump, Danny Dial-a-Flow, Ellie Infusion Ball): turn medical tools into friendly companions so Penny feels safe.
- **Parents/caregivers:** loving, supportive, encourage her bravery; present but not the main focus.

## Turnaround (360°) guide

- **Front:** pigtails visible and even, heart-pattern dress centered, sneakers matching pair, soft round proportions, warm brown eyes forward.
- **Side:** slight tummy curve, dress flares gently, near pigtail curves backward, sneaker profile rounded and simple.
- **Back:** heart pattern continues across the dress, pigtails symmetrical, hem slightly curved, bows visible from behind.
- **¾ view (most expressive, default for covers/key art):** often shown holding or hugging Benny Bear, gentle cheek curve, sweet curious smile.

## Pose library

Sitting cross-legged on the rug · hugging Benny Bear · holding Nurse Ivy's hand · taking a deep breath (calming down) · standing brave (small hands on hips, chin up) · nervous (hands together, shoulders up, holding Benny tight) · asking a question (head tilt) · jumping/bouncing excitedly · looking out the window · peaceful/sleepy curled with Benny.
