# Sales Discovery Intake Form

## What this is

A structured capture sheet for Sales Partners to fill out during or immediately after a discovery conversation with a prospect. It turns the conversational discovery questions in `05_ICP_AND_DISCOVERY.md` into a fillable form so the same information lands in the CRM consistently every time, in the required note format from `07_CRM_SOP.md` Section 4.

This is a different document from `18_VOICE_AGENT_CALLER_INTAKE_TEMPLATE.md` (what the client's AI agent asks the client's own end customers) and from `06_OUTBOUND_CALL_SCRIPT.md` (the approved cold-call script). This form is for MCD's own sales conversations with prospective business owners.

## After the call

Enter this information into the CRM using the required note format (`07_CRM_SOP.md` Section 4) and set the pipeline stage to match the actual outcome — do not advance a stage to make the pipeline look better than it is. If a DNC/opt-out request came up, log it immediately per Section 7.
